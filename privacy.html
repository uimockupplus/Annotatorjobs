Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 28, 2026
IN SHORTWe collect very little. If you email us your resume or chat with our assistant, we use that only to help you find relevant opportunities. We do not sell your personal information. Third-party platforms you visit through our links have their own privacy policies.

01Who we are

EvalLoopjobs (“we”, “us”) runs the EvalLoopjobs website (the “Site”), a curated directory of opportunities in AI training, annotation, evaluation, and related expert work. This policy explains what information we handle and why. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

02Information we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. Depending on how you use the Site, we may receive:

  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as your resume, target roles, location, and work type preferences.
  • Chatbot messages: if you use the AI chatbot, we (and the service that powers it) receive the messages you type.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log details such as IP address, browser type, device, and pages visited, for security and reliability.

The Site does not have accounts or sign-up forms, and we do not ask for payment details.

03How we use it

  • To read your resume and preferences so we can suggest more relevant opportunities;
  • To reply to your emails and questions;
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site and the chatbot;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sending us your resume is voluntary, and we are not obliged to reply or to find you a role. We do not sell your personal information.

04AI chatbot

The Site may include an AI-powered chatbot. Messages you type may be processed by a third-party AI service in order to generate replies. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat, such as ID numbers, bank details, passwords, or health information.

05Third-party links and services

Many links on the Site lead to platforms we do not own or control, and some are referral links. Once you leave the Site, that platform’s own terms and privacy policy apply, and we are not responsible for how it handles your data. The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers when a page loads. When you email us, your message passes through your email provider and ours.

06Cookies

We do not use cookies to show you advertising. Your browser or our hosting provider may use essential technical storage needed to load and secure the Site. If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will update this policy.

07Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the Site, email, and chatbot, under their own terms;
  • if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the Site;
  • with your permission, for example if you ask us to pass your details to a platform or company.

08How long we keep it

We keep emails and resumes only as long as needed to help you or to meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete what you sent us at any time.

09Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no website or email system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please send only information you are comfortable sharing.

10Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it, by emailing us at techultron2020.plus@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where it applies.

11Children

The Site is intended for adults looking for work. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please tell us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version, and continued use of the Site means you accept the update.

13Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at techultron2020.plus@gmail.com.
