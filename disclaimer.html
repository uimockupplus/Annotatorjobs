A clear word about opportunities.

Last updated: September 28, 2026
IN SHORT EvalLoop Jobs is an opportunity discovery platform. We share information and links to AI-related work, but we do not employ contributors, guarantee acceptance or income, or control third-party platforms.

01Information, not employment

EvalLoop Jobs is not an employer, staffing agency, recruitment agency, or contracting company. Listing an opportunity on the Site does not create an employment, contractor, agency, partnership, or other working relationship between EvalLoop Jobs and an applicant.

02Third-party opportunities

Many opportunities listed on EvalLoop Jobs are offered by third-party companies, platforms, contributor networks, or project operators.

Those organizations independently control their application processes, eligibility requirements, onboarding, work allocation, rates, payment terms, account decisions, and project availability.

03No guarantee

We do not guarantee that an opportunity will remain open, that you will qualify, that you will be selected, that work will be available after onboarding, or that you will earn a particular amount.

Compensation, requirements, deadlines, geographic eligibility, project availability, and platform rules may change after an opportunity is published.

04Verify before applying

Before submitting personal information or accepting work, verify the opportunity directly through the official company or platform.

  • Check the current job description and eligibility requirements.
  • Confirm the current compensation and payment terms.
  • Check whether the opportunity is available in your country or region.
  • Review the platform's current terms and privacy policy.
  • Use the official application or onboarding process.

05Referral links

Some links on EvalLoop Jobs may be referral or tracked links. If you use a referral link, the relevant third-party platform may record that referral under its own systems and terms.

The presence of a referral link does not change the platform's own application, selection, onboarding, payment, or eligibility decisions.

06Opportunity accuracy

We try to publish useful and relevant information, but listings can become outdated or change without notice. A listing on EvalLoop Jobs should not be treated as confirmation that a role is still accepting applications.

If you notice an opportunity that is closed, incorrect, misleading, or potentially unsafe, please contact us so we can review it.

07Scams and suspicious requests

Be cautious if an opportunity asks you for money, unusual payments, passwords, banking credentials, or sensitive identity information before legitimate onboarding.

When in doubt, stop and verify the opportunity directly with the official company or platform.

08AI-generated information

EvalLoop Jobs may use AI-assisted tools for research, organization, search, or chatbot responses. AI-generated information can contain errors. Important information should always be checked against the relevant official source.

09Contact

For corrections, suspicious listings, or questions about this disclaimer, contact evalloopjobs@gmail.com.
