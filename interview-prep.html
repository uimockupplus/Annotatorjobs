Understand our question sources

We distinguish candidate-reported interview experiences from verified sources and practice questions.

Candidate-reported

Questions reported as part of a candidate's interview experience. This label does not mean EvalLoop Jobs has independently verified the interview or received confirmation from the company.

Verified source

Used when an entry has a qualifying verification status or verified source classification. Check the original source, when available, for context.

Practice question

Questions intended to help candidates practise relevant concepts. They should not be interpreted as actual questions asked by a specific company.
