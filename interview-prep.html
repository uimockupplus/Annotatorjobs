A BETTER APPROACH

How to use these questions

Practise explaining concepts clearly instead of memorizing answers word for word.

01

Understand the concept

Learn why a method works, when it is useful, its limitations, and how it compares with alternatives.

02

Practise your answer

Explain your approach step by step. For technical questions, include assumptions, trade-offs, evaluation criteria, and practical examples.

03

Prepare for follow-ups

Be ready to explain why you chose a solution, how you would test it, what could fail, and how you would improve it.

TRANSPARENCY

Know what each question represents

Practice Question: An illustrative question created to help candidates prepare. It is not presented as a question verified to have been asked by a specific company.

Candidate-Reported: A question reported by a candidate or another identifiable source. Where available, the source and relevant context should be provided. A report does not independently guarantee that the question is representative of every interview at that company.

Verified Source: Material supported by an identifiable source. The source should be linked where possible, and claims should not exceed what the source actually establishes.
