PREPARATION METHOD / 04

Practise beyond memorisation.

Strong interview preparation means understanding the reasoning behind an answer and explaining how you would apply it to a real problem.

STEP / 01

Understand the concept.

Learn how a method works, when to use it, its limitations, and how it compares with alternative approaches.

STEP / 02

Explain your approach.

Describe your assumptions, implementation steps, evaluation criteria, trade-offs, and practical examples.

STEP / 03

Prepare for follow-ups.

Practise explaining why you chose a solution, how you would test it, what could fail, and how you would improve it.

SOURCE TRANSPARENCY / 05

Know the source behind a question.

Practice Question
An illustrative question created to help candidates prepare. It is not a claim that a particular employer has asked that exact question.

Candidate-Reported
A question reported by a candidate or an identifiable source. Where available, the original source and relevant context should be linked. A report does not establish that every interview at that company uses the same question.

Verified Source
A question supported by an identifiable source. Source links and verification details should be included where available. A source should never be represented as proving more than it actually does.
