Put your profile in the loop.

Join the EvalLoop contributor network by sharing your background, skills, languages and the type of AI work you are interested in.

What this is for.

The network is intended to help us understand what kinds of contributors are interested in AI work.

Your information may help us identify relevant opportunities, future projects, or contributor needs.

Submitting your profile does not guarantee a job, project, interview, acceptance, or paid work.

Useful signals.

  • AI evaluation experience
  • Annotation experience
  • Languages you know
  • Domain expertise
  • Remote / onsite preference
  • Portfolio or LinkedIn
Privacy note: Only share information you are comfortable providing. Do not include passwords, government ID numbers, bank information, or other sensitive information.
For now, use a shareable resume link. Make sure the file can be viewed by the intended recipient.
