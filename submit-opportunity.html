What to submit

AI training, model evaluation, data annotation, expert work, language and speech projects, GenAI roles, AI agent projects, and related opportunities.

✓

Before you submit

Help us verify the opportunity by providing:

  • 1
    A genuine company or opportunity provider.
  • 2
    An official application link whenever possible.
  • 3
    Accurate requirements, work mode, and compensation details when available.
◎

Independent directory

EvalLoop Jobs is not the employer or hiring company for submitted opportunities. We review and organize listings to help people discover them. Applications are made through the original provider's channel.
