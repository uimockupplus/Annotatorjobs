01 WHY WE EXIST

THE OPPORTUNITY GAP

AI work is everywhere. Discovery is still fragmented.

Opportunities in AI can appear on company career pages, contributor platforms, project boards, specialist communities, and recruitment websites. Finding relevant work across these different sources takes time and effort.

EvalLoop Jobs brings these opportunities together in one curated discovery experience, helping people explore different paths into the AI ecosystem.

↗ OUR PLATFORM MODEL

A discovery platform. Not the employer.

EvalLoop Jobs is an independent directory for discovering AI-related opportunities. We help people find opportunities published by companies, hiring platforms, project providers, contributor networks, and other public sources.

The organization or provider identified in each listing controls its opportunity and application process. Applications should be submitted through the original application channel.
