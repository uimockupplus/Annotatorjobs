02 / WHAT WE DO

An independent directory for AI opportunities.

EvalLoop Jobs is an independent opportunity discovery platform. We curate and organize opportunities from companies, hiring platforms, project providers, contributor networks, and other publicly available sources.

EvalLoop Jobs is not the employer or project provider for the opportunities listed on this website. The organization identified in an individual listing is the associated employer or opportunity provider, based on the available source information.

Candidates can follow the original application channel when one is available. Always verify the latest requirements, eligibility, compensation, deadlines, and application process with the original source.

03 / THE PROBLEM

AI work is growing. Discovery is fragmented.

Relevant opportunities are spread across company career pages, hiring platforms, contributor communities, specialist networks, and project boards.

Finding suitable work can take time, particularly when similar opportunities appear under different job titles or across multiple platforms.

EvalLoop Jobs brings these opportunities together in one place, helping people discover relevant work and navigate to the original application source.

07 / TRANSPARENCY

Clear attribution. Original sources.

Each listing identifies the associated company, organization, or opportunity provider whenever that information is available.

For example, an opportunity from AlphaSense remains attributed to AlphaSense, while an opportunity from WonderBotz remains attributed to WonderBotz. EvalLoop Jobs serves as the discovery layer that brings opportunities together.

Listings may change, close, or become outdated. The original source should be treated as authoritative for current availability, eligibility, requirements, compensation, and application instructions.

Candidates should exercise appropriate caution before sharing personal information, paying fees, completing assessments, or accepting an offer.
