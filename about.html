01 / OUR ROLE

An opportunity directory, not a recruiting agency.

EvalLoop Jobs is an independent opportunity discovery platform. We collect, curate, and organize publicly available opportunities from companies, hiring platforms, project providers, contributor networks, and other relevant sources.

EvalLoop Jobs is not the employer or project provider for the opportunities listed in the directory. The organization identified in each listing is the associated employer or opportunity provider, as indicated by the available source information.

Whenever an original application link is available, candidates can use it to review the opportunity and follow the provider's application process. Always verify the latest requirements, eligibility, compensation, deadlines, and terms with the original source.

02 / THE PROBLEM

AI opportunities are everywhere. Discovery is fragmented.

Relevant work can be scattered across company career pages, specialist platforms, contributor communities, project boards, and hiring networks.

Finding the right opportunity can take time, especially when different roles use different titles for similar skills.

EvalLoop Jobs brings opportunities from across the AI ecosystem into one place, helping people discover relevant work and navigate to its original source.

06 / TRANSPARENCY

Clear sources. Clear attribution.

Every opportunity should make it easier to understand which organization is associated with the role and where to find the original listing.

For example, an opportunity associated with AlphaSense remains attributed to AlphaSense, while an opportunity associated with WonderBotz remains attributed to WonderBotz. EvalLoop Jobs serves as the discovery layer, not a replacement for those organizations or their application processes.

Listings may change, close, or become outdated. We encourage candidates to verify every opportunity through its original source before sharing personal information, completing assessments, or accepting an offer.
