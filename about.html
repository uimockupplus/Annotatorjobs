A clearer path into the AI work economy.

EvalLoop Jobs is an independent opportunity discovery platform for people looking for work across AI training, annotation, evaluation, GenAI, agentic systems, expert review, voice and data projects, and related fields.


What EvalLoop Jobs is.

EvalLoop Jobs is an independent directory and discovery platform. We curate and organize opportunities from companies, hiring platforms, project providers, contributor networks, and other publicly available sources.

EvalLoop Jobs is not the employer for the opportunities listed on this website. The company or organization named on an individual opportunity is the associated employer or opportunity provider.

Applications are handled through the original application channel whenever one is available. Candidates should review the original source, company, requirements, eligibility, compensation, and application process before applying.

Why EvalLoop exists.

AI work is growing quickly, but finding legitimate opportunities can still mean searching across many different platforms, communities, company pages, and project boards.

EvalLoop Jobs brings those signals into one place so contributors can spend less time hunting and more time understanding the opportunity itself.

The goal is simple: make useful AI-work opportunities easier to discover, compare, and reach through their original application channels.

How opportunity attribution works.

Each listing identifies the company, organization, or opportunity provider associated with that opportunity whenever the source information is available.

For example, an opportunity from AlphaSense remains attributed to AlphaSense, while an opportunity from WonderBotz remains attributed to WonderBotz. EvalLoop Jobs acts as the discovery layer that presents these opportunities in one directory.

The original application source should always be treated as the authoritative source for the current status, requirements, eligibility, and application process.
